# Trip.com

## Overview of Trip.com: Services and Features

Trip.com is an all-in-one travel platform designed to meet the diverse needs of travelers worldwide. As a leading international travel service provider, we offer comprehensive one-stop travel solutions for customers around the globe. Our platform is backed by a strong global network and provides seamless travel experiences with access to flights, hotels, car rentals, trains, vacation packages, and more.

With services available in **24 languages** and offering **local currencies** in **39 countries and regions**, Trip.com makes travel easy, accessible, and affordable for everyone — whether you're planning a quick getaway or an international adventure.

Our platform is designed to cater to travelers worldwide, helping them find the best deals and navigate their travel plans with ease, no matter where they are.

 ### Flight Search and Booking
- [Flights Homepage](https://www.trip.com/flights/): The homepage for Trip.com Flights, offering search options and showcasing popular flight products. This serves as a good starting point for users looking to book flights.
- **Matching URL**: `^https://www\.trip\.com/flights(/[\w\-]+)*(/[\w\-]+)?/?$`
    - **Function**: All pages related to flight products, including flight search, flight status, booking options, and flight tickets. These pages offer users a wide range of flight-related services and information.
- **Matching URL**: `^https://www\.trip\.com/flights/([a-zA-Z]+)-to-([a-zA-Z]+)/airfares-[a-zA-Z]{3}-[a-zA-Z]{3}/$`
    - **Function**: This is the search results page for selected flight routes, including one-way and round-trip tickets, flight details, and related FAQs. Users can find relevant flight information and proceed with their booking.
- **Matching URL**: `^https://www\.trip\.com/flights/status-[a-zA-Z]{1,3}\d{2,4}/$`
    - **Function**: Displays the real-time status of selected flights, allowing users to check live flight information and adjust their travel plans accordingly.

- **Matching URL**: `^https://www\.trip\.com/hotels/([a-zA-Z\-]+)-hotels-list-(\d+)/$`
